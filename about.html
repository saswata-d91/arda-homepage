INNOVATION WITH PURPOSE

OPERATIONAL DEPTH

An End-to-End Approach to Revenue Cycle Management

Detailed breakdown of our five core operational execution phases.

01 • PATIENT ACCESS

Eligibility & Benefits Verification

Proactive real-time batch verification checking active insurance coverage, copay requirements, deductible status, and prior authorization needs before patient arrival.

02 • AUTHORIZATIONS

Authorization Management

Dedicated tracking and submission of prior authorization requests, reducing procedure delays and preventing retroactive claim denials.

03 • BILLING & CODING

Medical Billing & Coding

Certified coders (CPC, CCS) ensuring ICD-10 and CPT precision, paired with electronic clearinghouse scrubbing for maximum clean claim rates.

04 • RECOVERY

Accounts Receivable Management

Systematic aging bucket stratification (>90, >120 days), stubborn insurance follow-up escalation, and proactive bad debt mitigation.

05 • RECONCILIATION

Payment Posting & Reconciliation

Accurate electronic remittance advice (ERA) posting, contractual adjustment verification, and daily ledger balancing.
