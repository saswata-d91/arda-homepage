WHO WE SUPPORT

Revenue operations across different care settings.

Organizations have different service lines, reimbursement pressures, and patient access patterns. Our support starts with those realities.

HOSPITALS & HEALTH SYSTEMS

Multi-Facility & Inpatient Operations

Large-scale healthcare systems face intricate inpatient DRG coding, complex charge master maintenance, and multi-facility compliance requirements. CuraSync provides scalable billing support and rigorous audit defense.

CLINICS & AMBULATORY CARE

High-Velocity Outpatient Workflows

Ambulatory care centers require rapid patient check-in, real-time eligibility verification, and low days-in-AR. We streamline front-end intake to keep patient flow and reimbursement moving smoothly.

PHYSICIAN PRACTICES

Specialty-Specific Revenue Care

From primary care to surgical subspecialties, physician practices benefit from meticulous CPT coding, provider credentialing oversight, and empathetic patient statement management.

DIAGNOSTIC & IMAGING CENTERS

Modality & Prior Auth Management

Diagnostic facilities depend on flawless prior authorizations and high-volume electronic claim scrubbing to prevent costly recurring rejections for high-cost imaging modalities.
